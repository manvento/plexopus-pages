01 / WHY PLEXOPUS

A map tells you where.
Plexopus helps you see how.

Knowing where a pipe or a valve is located is only part of the picture. Teams also need to know what connects to what, which relationships are valid, and how operating conditions change the paths through a network.

01

A shared understanding of the network

Bring assets, connections and operating states into a consistent model. Give GIS specialists, data managers and operations teams a clearer picture of the same network.

02

Data you can check

Identify connections that break your rules and inconsistencies in asset data. Turn scattered checks into a clearer understanding of network quality.

03

Freedom to choose your tools

Develop network intelligence around an open GIS ecosystem. Keep control of your data and choose a deployment that fits your organisation.

02 / WHAT YOU CAN DO

From asset records
to network understanding.

Plexopus brings together the fundamentals of utility network management. These capabilities form the current development baseline.

Model your assets

Describe asset types, their properties and the ways they can connect. Organise a network around the rules of your service.

Explore connectivity

Find assets reachable from a starting point. Follow connections while accounting for operating states, such as an open or closed valve.

Validate network data

Check network connections and asset information against your model. Find issues that need attention before they undermine analysis.

Work with existing data

Bring supported utility network datasets into Plexopus, including a conversion path for Esri Utility Network data.

04 / THE ROAD AHEAD

A clear direction.
Room to grow.

An overview of the project’s direction. Future stages are indicative and carry no delivery dates or commitments.

  1. Current development

    Foundation

    Asset modelling, connectivity, rule-based validation and connected tracing, with a first focus on water networks.

  2. Next priorities

    Consolidation

    Access control, more controlled updates and continued work on reliability and usability.

  3. Future direction

    Expansion

    Richer network analysis, closer GIS integration, collaborative workflows and additional utility profiles.
